3. $|x + 3| + |x - 1| \geq 5$ \\

Si igualam els valors absoluts a 0, treum dues arrels, tals que: $x = -3$ i $x = 1$
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Cas 1: $x < -3$}\\
$-(x + 3) - (x - 1) \geq 5 \implies -x - 3 - x + 1 \geq 5 \implies -2x \geq 7 \implies x \leq -3.5$ \\
Treim Sol.1: $x \in (-\infty, -3.5]$
\item \textbf{Cas 2: $-3 \leq x < 1$} \\
$(x + 3) - (x - 1) \geq 5 \implies x + 3 - x + 1 \geq 5 \implies 4 \geq 5$ (Fals) \\
Treim Sol.2: $\emptyset$
\item \textbf{Cas 3: $x \geq 1$} \\
$(x + 3) + (x - 1) \geq 5 \implies x + 3 + x - 1 \geq 5 \implies 2x \geq 3 \implies x \geq 1.5$ \\
Treim Sol.3: $[1.5, +\infty)$
\end{itemize}
Si unim les tres solucions, tenim que: $x \in (-\infty, -3.5] \cup [1.5, +\infty)$\\
